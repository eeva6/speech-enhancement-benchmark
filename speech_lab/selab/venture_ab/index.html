Conversation Bubble: before/after on real 7-mic recordings

Real recordings of LibriSpeech talkers replayed over loudspeakers in a real meeting room (LibriCSS), captured by a 7-mic table array (8.5 cm across). Every number on this page was measured on these clips. Clips were picked by a fixed random rule from held-out sessions, not hand-picked (one extra clip is labelled BEST CASE). This is captured audio, not what reaches anyone's ear.

What is and is not claimed

A Captured-audio improvement: what the mic array plus processing does to the recorded signal. Measured.

B Target vs competing talker(s): change in the target-to-other-talker ratio, measured separately from noise.

C ANC at the ear: SIMULATED only (lab simulation curves, not a device or head).

D Open-room reduction: NOT claimed. Nothing here makes the room quieter for anyone. Processing changes only the signal the wearer would be fed.

ORACLE modes use information only the lab has (true talker direction or true signals). They are upper bounds, not products.
